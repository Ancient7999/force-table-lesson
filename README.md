# Force table

An interactive lesson on the addition of vectors with a force table.

- **Practical**: level the table with its spirit level, hang slotted masses over three pulleys, set the angles, and adjust the third force until the ring is centred and the thread above it hangs straight. Readings go into an observation table, the resultant is drawn to scale head to tail on a drawing board, and a step-by-step calculation compares the theoretical, experimental and graphical values.
- **Aim and outcomes**, **Theory**, **Method**, **Observation and calculation** (a worked example) and **MCQs**.
- A guided tutorial walks through the whole practical, with adjustable speed and step controls.
